adaawakdi

(To tell a story)

Naa ńiinis?

(Who are you?)

Ẃii Noxhl waý ii Ksim laxsgiik ńiiý
Wilps Sim'oogit Gwiix Ḿaaẃ wil witgwiý
Lheidli T'enneh wil jogaý
Nisga'a ńiiý

Hello, my name is Terra Barton, my Nisga'a name is Big Mama and I come from the Eagle tribe. The house I am from belongs to Chief Gwiix Ḿaaẃ. I live in the traditional territory of the Lheidli T'enneh First Nation, which is located in what is now known as Prince George, British Columbia. I am Nisga'a.

Terra Barton is a multi-talented creative powerhouse who masterfully bridges the worlds of cutting-edge technology, contemporary media, and deeply rooted ancestral traditions. As a multimedia graphic design artist, book illustrator, and writer, she weaves complex narratives visually and textually, translating rich cultural imagery and contemporary design concepts across both digital landscapes and physical pages. Her profound connection to heritage shines through her work as a nontraditional formline artist, knowledge keeper, and intricate beader of hats and jewelry, preserving and reshaping indigenous art forms for modern audiences while honoring ancient roots.

This creative expression is seamlessly balanced by her analytical prowess as a videographer and GIS/GPS technician, roles in which she uses data-driven precision, mapping technology, and moving image to document stories and capture the shifting dimensions of the world around her. Whether she is designing a digital portfolio, drafting local geometric patterns, or beads onto a brim, Terra stands as a passionate crafter whose diverse skills breathe life into community storytelling.
